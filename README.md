# About us:
<p>Advanced Headache Center is a headache and <a href="https://www.advancedheadachecenter.com/">migraine treatment center</a> at 41 5th Avenue in Greenwich Village, New York, NY. Led by Dr. Amr Hosny, MD, MBA, FASA, the center provides diagnostic evaluation and personalized care for patients with frequent, severe, chronic, or complex headaches and migraines.</p>
<p><br>Dr. Hosny is a board-certified headache medicine specialist with advanced training in headache care, interventional pain management, and complex head pain conditions. He completed residency training at St. Luke&rsquo;s-Roosevelt Hospital Center, affiliated with Columbia University in New York City, and fellowship training at Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center, part of Harvard Medical School in Boston. He has been recognized by Castle Connolly as a New York Top Doctor and is affiliated with leading headache medicine organizations, including the National Headache Foundation, the American Headache Society, and the International Headache Society.</p>
<p><br><a href="https://www.google.com/maps?cid=9558629552742217445">Advanced Headache Center</a> uses a diagnosis-first, multimodal approach to evaluate headache patterns, review medical and family history, assess previous treatments, identify possible triggers, and determine whether symptoms may be related to primary headache disorders, nerve irritation, musculoskeletal factors, allergies, medication overuse, hormonal changes, nutrient deficiencies, or other underlying causes. Evaluation may include clinical examination, laboratory testing, allergy testing, and radiology services when appropriate.</p>
<p><br>The center treats migraine, chronic migraine, chronic headaches, tension headaches, cluster headaches, occipital neuralgia, cervicogenic headaches, post-concussion headaches, trigeminal neuralgia, rebound headaches, sinus-related headaches, and other head, neck, and facial pain conditions.</p>
<p><br>Core treatment options may include migraine medication management, rational polypharmacy, trigger point injections, occipital nerve blocks, sphenopalatine ganglion block, stellate ganglion block, intravenous infusions, acupuncture, chiropractic care, physical therapy, and integrative headache care. The practice uses a coordinated care model that combines medical evaluation, interventional treatment options, rehabilitation support, and prevention-focused planning when appropriate.</p>
<p><br>The Greenwich Village office serves patients from Union Square, NoHo, Ukrainian Village, Peter Cooper Village, East Village, Flatiron District, Lower Manhattan, and nearby New York City communities. To schedule an appointment with Advanced Headache Center in NYC, call 646-763-2222 or visit <a href="https://www.advancedheadachecenter.com/">https://www.advancedheadachecenter.com/</a>.</p>
